## 2026-08-18 — localization descoped; VELDE gets a real accent

| Proposed | Done instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (me) KRACHT ships in Dutch; locale becomes a fourth brand axis alongside colour, type and personification | **Both stores ship English. | (me) VELDE's accent is near-black `#1C1B19`, faithful to how minimal-lux stores really ship | **Ink-blue `#2C3E5C`** | Measured: black derives to roughly `textPrimary` on a paper surface, so the config page's accent control — the merchant-facing demo of "accent drives the brand" — looked inert on brand A, and the focus ring on VELDE's own accent-filled CTA computed **1.0:1**. Ink-blue is 10.32:1 on surface, flips `textOnAccent` to white at 10.77:1, keeps the restrained read, and leaves the control visibly doing something.
