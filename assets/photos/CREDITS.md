# Photo credits

## VELDE

Real photography, sourced from Europeana, Smithsonian Open Access and Wikimedia Commons under
licences permitting **commercial use and modification** (CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA).
No CC licence with an `NC` or `ND` clause is used anywhere in this set.

Every image was processed identically so the set reads as one shoot: the original ground is
white-balanced onto VELDE's paper-white `#FBFAF8` and flattened, the subject is cropped, padded
to a 1000x1000 square at a fixed subject scale, then a single neutral grade (saturation 0.86,
contrast 1.06) is applied across the whole set. A few frames were cropped first to remove a
museum colour-reference chart or a dress-form base. JPEG q78; the whole VELDE set is ~1.1 MB.

**This set is 20 images, not the ~32 the catalog can show.** That is deliberate. Candidates were
filtered mechanically (uniform light studio ground, in-palette colour, resolution, near-duplicate
hash) and then judged as a grid rather than one at a time; what shipped is what cleared the bar.
Anything with a human subject, a visible third-party brand mark, a non-neutral background, or a
colour outside black / charcoal / grey / ecru / navy / natural leather brown was rejected even
when the garment itself was good.

## KRACHT — generated, not photographed

**These 34 images are original SVG artwork produced by `assets/photos/generate_kracht.py`, not photography.**
No external source, no licence required, nothing to attribute. They exist because no open-licensed
real photography of a supplement tub can carry KRACHT's own label — every real packshot on Commons,
Europeana or Smithsonian carries a different, real brand's mark, which would read as that brand's
product rather than KRACHT's.

Each render is a matte-black tub on neutral studio grey with a soft blurred contact shadow, an acid
lime (`#C6F441`) lid, the KRACHT wordmark, a per-line sub-label, a flavour name with a flavour-coded
swatch, and a macro line. The printed sub-labels and flavours are driven by
`apps/shop-kracht/data/products.json` so the images and the catalog agree. No size or servings line
is printed: one image serves several size variants and the storefront renders the size itself.

Regenerate with `python3 assets/photos/generate_kracht.py` (Python + cairosvg + Pillow).
